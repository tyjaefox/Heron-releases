# Heron

Persistent sticky notes for Windows. Instant to open, invisible when idle, entirely local.

Heron is a native notes app for people who want a note on screen faster than they can think of what to write. Written purely in C++, it consistently uses under 10 MB of memory, opens with no splash screen or warmup, and functions as a tool, not a service. No login, no cloud sync, no uploads. It is simple, elegant, and is excels at being exactly what it claims to be.

## What it does

**Simple by design**  
Everything you need, nothing you don't.

**Instant and lightweight**  
Fast, responsive, and designed to keep system usage low.

**Notes that persist**  
Every note stays exactly where you left it, how you left it.

**Always within reach**  
Keep notes convenient without cluttering your desktop.

**Appearance themes**  
Eight built-in colour schemes, switchable per note.

**System tray integration**  
Lives in the tray, out of the taskbar, one click from anywhere.

**Familiar, refined**  
Exceptional performance with a familiar feel.
